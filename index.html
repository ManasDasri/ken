Clause Risk Review

Does legal pretraining help a model find risky contract clauses?

Six kinds of risky clause across 510 lawyer-annotated contracts (CUAD). A TextCNN, BERT and Legal-BERT train on identical windows and per-contract splits; every threshold is tuned on validation data only, and 90 tests pin each of those choices.

BERT: 0.879 document F1 on 76 held-out contracts. Legal-BERT: no measurable gain, and the write-up says so.

Med-EEG

Can two EEG numbers track a meditator’s focus?

A retrospective study on open meditation EEG: an MNE pipeline from raw signal to features, person-held-out models checked against shuffled labels and non-EEG baselines, and a pre-registered replication on a second dataset. NeuroDial shows the latest published run.

The expert-vs-novice result turned out to be confounded by sex, the replication didn’t confirm, and meditation depth decoded at AUC 0.609. All reported.

Forme

Components for interfaces that glow.

53 free motion components for React (shaders, ASCII scenes, HUD instruments, inputs, loaders) that you install as source you own, with one shadcn command.

Each component’s accessibility badges are tests: CI fails if a claimed behaviour has no evidence in the code.

Flow

Study until the sun sets.

A study room you share with friends: start a focus block and a sun sinks for everyone in the room; when it sets, you all take a break. Peer-to-peer video over WebRTC, shared tasks, synced music, and an AI in the chat.

v1.0 released November 2026. Free, and the code is open.

Velora

Probability cones for stock prices.

Instead of one price target, Velora simulates thousands of price paths with regime switching and jump shocks, and shows the spread: fan charts, upside probability, VaR and CVaR. Charts are drawn on a plain canvas, no charting library.
