  1. Clause Risk Review

    2026 · PyTorch, Transformers, 510 contracts

    Does legal pretraining help a model find risky contract clauses? TextCNN, BERT and Legal-BERT, trained on identical windows and per-contract splits, every threshold tuned on validation only.

    BERT reached 0.879 document F1 on 76 held-out contracts. Legal-BERT gave no measurable gain, and the write-up says so.

    ResultsCode

  2. Med-EEG

    2026 · Python, MNE, scikit-learn, 64-channel EEG

    Can two EEG numbers track a meditator’s focus? Person-held-out models, checked against shuffled labels and non-EEG baselines, with a pre-registered replication on a second dataset.

    The expertise result was confounded by sex and the replication didn’t confirm; depth decoded at AUC 0.609. All reported.

    NeuroDialCode
